Lightower's Optical Encryption service utilizes revolutionary technology to offer FIPS-certified encryption of in-flight data from end-point to end-point. Operating at Layer 1, the Physical Layer, the encryption is data agnostic, while maximizing network throughput by eliminating the encryption headers used at higher layers like Ethernet or Internet Protocol. Encryption shouldn't slow your network down, our Layer 1 approach adds zero protocol overhead.

Since Lightower offers Optical Encryption as a service, there is no need to purchase, deploy, and manage equipment. End users maintain total control over the most important parts, their own security parameters and security keys.
